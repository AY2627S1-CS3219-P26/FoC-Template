Title: How to change your password
Questions: How do I change my password? How do I update my password? Will I be logged out if I change my password? What does "is wrong" mean on the password form?

1. Open Profile.
2. In the Change password section, enter your current password and a new password of 8 to 128 characters.
3. Press Change password. You see the message "Password changed".

You stay logged in on the device you are using, and every other device is logged out. If the form says the current password "is wrong", nothing was changed and what you typed stays in the form.
