Title: Problems logging in
Questions: Why can't I log in? What does "Wrong username, email, or password" mean? What does "Please verify your email address first" mean? What does "This account is suspended" mean?

"Wrong username, email, or password": one of them is incorrect. The message does not say which, for security reasons. Check for typing mistakes and that you are using your NUS email.
"Please verify your email address first": your account exists but the code was never entered. Follow the Verify your email link under the message and enter your code, or press Send a new code there.
"This account is suspended": an administrator has suspended the account and you cannot log in. You cannot lift this yourself.

If you forgot your password, see the document on forgotten passwords.
