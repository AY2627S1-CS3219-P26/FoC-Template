Title: How to create an account
Questions: How do I sign up? How do I register? How do I create an account? How do I get started?

1. Open the Log in page and choose Create an account, or go to /signup.
2. Fill in Full name, NUS email, Username and Password.
3. Press Create account.
4. You are taken to the Verify your email page, and a 6-digit code is sent to your NUS email.
5. Enter the code and press Verify.
6. Log in with your password.

You cannot log in until your email is verified.
