Title: How to verify your email
Questions: How do I verify my account? Where do I enter the code? Where is my verification code? I did not get the email. How do I get a new code?

After signing up you land on the Verify your email page. Enter your NUS email and the 6-digit code from the email, then press Verify. You then go to the Log in page with your email filled in, and only need your password.

If no code arrived, check your spam folder and that you typed the right email, then press Send a new code on the same page. If the page shows a notice saying "Test mode: no real email is sent", open the test inbox linked in that notice and copy your code from there.

If you left the page, log in with your email and password, then follow the Verify your email link.
