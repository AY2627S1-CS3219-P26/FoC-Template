Title: What works in the admin console
Questions: How do I see all students? How do I manage users as admin? How do I suspend an account? How do I make someone an administrator? How do I resolve a dispute? How do I add a supplier as admin? How do I deactivate a supplier? How do I decide a report? How do I see orders as admin? Where do I see all credit movements?

Students lists every account. Open one to see its details, state history, reports and credit balance, to suspend or reinstate it with a reason of 20 to 500 characters, or to make it an administrator or remove that role. You cannot edit or delete an account from this page.

Reports lists reports by state: Open, Dismissed or Actioned. Open a report and give a justification of 20 to 500 characters, then press Dismiss report or Suspend account.

Suppliers lists suppliers. Press Add supplier to create one, or open one to edit it and to deactivate or reactivate it.

Credits lists every credit movement of every student, newest first, with its time, student, type, errand and amount. Search by a student's account id to see only theirs.

Orders and Disputes are not finished and currently show "Not built yet", so you cannot see orders or resolve disputes yet.
