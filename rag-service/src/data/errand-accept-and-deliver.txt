Title: How to accept and deliver an errand
Questions: How do I accept an errand? How do I deliver? How do I earn credits? How do I mark something as picked up? Why can't I accept this errand? Can I accept my own errand? Who confirms delivery? Can I give an errand back? Where are my deliveries?

The Errands page has three tabs: Browse open lists the open errands of other students, My requests the errands you posted, and My deliveries the errands you accepted.

1. In Browse open, press Accept errand on an errand. You are now its courier and it moves to My deliveries.
2. After collecting the items, press Mark picked up.
3. After handing them over, press Mark delivered.
4. The requester presses Confirm delivery / Pay courier, the errand becomes Completed and the credits are transferred to you.

An errand has only one courier, and only an Open errand can be accepted. You cannot accept it once someone else has, once its needed-by time has passed, or if it is your own. Only the courier can mark Picked up and Delivered, and only the requester can confirm. A courier cannot give an accepted errand back; if you cannot do it, tell the requester in the errand chat, since only the requester can cancel. Once an errand is accepted, only its requester and courier can see it.
