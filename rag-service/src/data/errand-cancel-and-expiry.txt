Title: Cancelled and expired errands
Questions: How do I cancel an errand? What happens if nobody accepts my errand? Do I get my credits back? What does expired mean? How long until an errand expires?

If nobody accepts your errand within the permitted time, it expires automatically. If an errand is cancelled or expires, the credits reserved for it are released back to your available balance, and no credits go to anyone else. You do not need to do anything to get them back.

Note: the errand pages are still being built, so the exact expiry time and the rules on when an errand can be cancelled are not available here yet.
