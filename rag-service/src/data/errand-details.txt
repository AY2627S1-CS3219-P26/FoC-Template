Title: The errand page
Questions: Where do I see the details of my errand? What does View details show? Who can see my errand? How do I see the progress of my errand? Where is the chat for my errand? What is my role on this errand?

Press View details on an errand card to open its page. It shows:
Progress: the steps from Posted to Confirmed and paid, with the current one marked.
Details: the pickup supplier, the drop-off location, the needed-by time, the items, the reward, your role (Requester or Courier), and when it was posted and last updated.
The chat with the other person on the errand, once a courier has accepted it.
Report a problem at the top, to report the other person on the errand.

Anyone can see an Open errand, because couriers browse them. Once it is accepted, only its requester and courier can open it; for everyone else it no longer exists.
