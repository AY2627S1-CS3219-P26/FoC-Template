Title: How to post an errand
Questions: How do I post an errand? How do I request something? How do I get something delivered? How do I create a request? Why was my errand refused?

Open Post an errand from the menu. Choose the pickup location from the campus suppliers and give the delivery details. Credits are reserved from your available balance when the errand is created, so you need enough available credits. The errand then shows as Open for other students to accept.

The app refuses an errand if the pickup supplier is inactive, or closed at the time you need the items by. Choose another supplier or a time when it is open.

Note: the errand pages are still being built, so button names and the exact fields may differ from this description.
