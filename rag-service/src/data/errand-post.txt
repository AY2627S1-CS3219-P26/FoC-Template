Title: How to post an errand
Questions: How do I post an errand? How do I request something? How do I get something delivered? How do I create a request? Why was my errand refused? How many items can I add? How many credits can I offer? How many credits should I offer? What does Suggest do?

1. Open Post an errand in the main menu.
2. Press Choose a pickup location and pick the store or facility. You can search by name and filter by type.
3. Enter the Drop-off location, for example COM3 or Kent Ridge MRT, then press Pick on map and mark the drop-off point. The point is required. Once both ends are set, the walk from the pickup is shown.
4. Set Needed by. It starts at the next full hour at least one hour from now.
5. Enter the Items, separated by commas, for example coffee, sandwich. An errand has 1 to 10 items.
6. Enter the Credits for the courier, from 1 to 50. Press Suggest to fill in a suggested reward from the usual rate for such an errand; you can still change it.
7. Press Post errand.

The credits are reserved from your available balance, so you need enough available credits. The errand shows "Reserving credits…" for a moment, then becomes Open for other students to accept. You find it on the Errands page under My requests.

An errand is refused if the needed-by time is in the past, or if the pickup supplier is inactive or closed at that time. The form names the field that is wrong. Choose another supplier or time.
