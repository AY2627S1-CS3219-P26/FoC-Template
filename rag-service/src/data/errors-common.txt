Title: Common error pages and messages
Questions: What does Access denied mean? What does Error 403 mean? What does Error 404 mean? Why do I see No connection or Temporarily unavailable? Why does the page say Not built yet? Something went wrong.

"Administrator access required" (Error 403): the page is for administrators and your account is not one.
"Access denied": you are not allowed to do this, or your session has ended. Try logging in again.
"Page not found" (Error 404): the address does not exist or has moved. Press Back to start.
"No connection": the app could not reach the server. Check your internet and press Try again.
"Temporarily unavailable": the server has a problem. Press Try again after a moment.
"Something went wrong": press Reload page.
"Not built yet": this page has not been finished.
