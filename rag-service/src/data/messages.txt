Title: Messages and chatting with other students
Questions: How do I message someone? How do I contact my courier? How do I talk to the requester? Where are my messages? What is the Relay conversation? Can I message the assistant from Messages?

Open Messages in the main menu to see your conversations with other students, newest first, with the number of unread messages. Open a conversation to read it and send a reply from the box at the bottom.

To start a conversation about an errand, press Message on the errand card on the Errands page, write your message and press Send message. It goes to the other person on that errand: the courier if it is your errand, otherwise the requester. The conversation then appears under Messages.

The conversation called Relay holds updates from the app itself. You cannot reply to it. To ask the assistant a question, use the round button in the bottom right corner instead.
