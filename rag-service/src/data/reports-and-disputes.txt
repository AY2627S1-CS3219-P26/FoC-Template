Title: Reporting a problem or disputing an errand
Questions: How do I report someone? How do I open a dispute? My courier did not deliver. The requester did not confirm. Who decides disputes? What if something goes wrong with my errand? What happens after I report someone?

A report names an errand and the other person on it, with a description of what happened of 20 to 500 characters. You can report an errand once. An administrator reads every report and either dismisses it or suspends the reported account.

The Report a problem page opens from an errand, and the errand pages are not finished yet, so you cannot send a report in the app yet. Keep the details of the errand so that you can describe what happened.

Administrators resolve disputes, but there is no way to open a dispute yet, and the Disputes page in the admin console is not finished either.
