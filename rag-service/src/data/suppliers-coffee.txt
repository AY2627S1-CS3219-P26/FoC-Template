Title: Coffee places on campus
Questions: Where can I get coffee? Which cafes are there? Is there coffee open at night? Where is TOMORO? Where is the Robot Cafe? Where is The Coffee Roaster?

Cafe+ Robot Cafe: Central Library, Level 1, opposite the library entrance. Central zone. Open 24 hours.
The Coffee Roaster: Blk AS8, Level 1, behind the central library bus stop. Central zone. Open 08:00 to 17:30.
TOMORO COFFEE: Hon Sui Sen Memorial Library, Level 2, inside HSSML. Business zone. Open 08:15 to 18:00.
Good Day Cafe: Medicine+Science Library, Level 1, inside the library. Medicine zone. Open 07:30 to 18:30.
he by He Brews: Engineering Block EA, Level 1, near LT7 and the Engineering Auditorium. Engineering zone. Open 08:00 to 17:00.
