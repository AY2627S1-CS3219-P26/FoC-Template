Title: Finding pickup locations
Questions: What is a supplier? Where can I pick up from? How do I search for a store? Which places are available? What stores are on the platform? Can I add a new store?

A supplier is a store, facility or landmark on campus where errands can be picked up from. Each has a name, a type (Food, Food/Coffee, Shopping or Printing), a campus zone, a building and address, a short description of how to find it, and opening hours. Filtering by Food does not include Food/Coffee, which is a type of its own.

Zones are Central, Computing, Science, Engineering, Medicine, Business and Residences. Search looks at the name, type, building, zone and address.

Only administrators can add or change suppliers, from the Suppliers page in the admin console. Students cannot add one.

Note: the student pages for browsing suppliers are not finished and currently show "Not built yet", but the assistant can still answer questions about the suppliers.
