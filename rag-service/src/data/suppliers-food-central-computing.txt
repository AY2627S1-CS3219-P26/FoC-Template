Title: Food in the Central and Computing zones
Questions: Where can I get food near the Central Library or YIH? Where can I eat near COM2 or COM3? Is there food open late at Computing? Where is Cool Spot? Where is InstaChef? Where is Smooy?

Anna's x Soup Union: Central Library, Level 1, next to NUS Co-op. Open 09:00 to 18:00.
Central Square @ YIH: Yusof Ishak House, Level 1, closest to the bus stop opposite UHC. Open 08:00 to 20:00.
Cool Spot: COM2, Level 1, opposite LT16. Open 09:00 to 21:30.
InstaChef: Terrace, Level 1, next to the foyer. Open 24 hours.
Smooy: COM3, Level 1, The Terrace @ COM3. Open 11:00 to 21:00.
