Title: Shops and convenience stores on campus
Questions: Where can I buy snacks or supplies? Is there a convenience store? Where is the NUS Co-op? Which shops are open 24 hours? Where is Octobox? Where is Cheers?

NUS Co-op: Central Library, Level 1, inside the library on the right side. Central zone. Open 09:00 to 16:00.
Octobox: Prince George's Park, Level 2, near the PGP entrance. Residences zone. Open 24 hours.
Cheers Unmanned Convenience Store: Engineering Block E3, Level 4, take a right from Arise and Shine. Engineering zone. Open 24 hours.
